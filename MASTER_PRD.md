# roomeo — Master PRD

Approved 27 October 2026. This document is the product. It replaces the 26 October 2026 PRD, earlier RoomSetu scope, and anything the current prototype does that is not written here. Approval of this document is not an order to rebuild.

**Market:** Bengaluru only.  
**Line:** People choose their own group. The owner says yes once. Each share is paid inside. No broker. No visit. No score.

## 1. Problem

Someone who cannot pay for a whole flat still needs a bed they can accept, in a shared flat or a small PG, with people they chose. A brand that assigns the person next to them, or locks the stay or the price, is not that.

An owner with empty beds will not give a broker a month's rent, will not interview time-wasters, and wants to say yes once to a group that is already formed. A small PG wants that same close.

If the seekers do not come, or the owner does not complete that yes, roomeo has failed.

## 2. What people do now

WhatsApp, Facebook, NoBroker, Housing, 99acres, and Magicbricks fail because the deal leaves the screen: a broker, cash, or a visit. A local broker is that behavior, not a separate rival.

A branded PG or co-living chain is a different enemy. It assigns who you live with, and it locks the stay or the price. Zolo and Stanza sit here. Putting them in the same basket as the portals was a mistake.

## 3. Promise

People form their own group. Nobody is assigned, and roomeo does not score who will get along. The owner sees that finished group and says yes once. Each person pays their own share inside the product. No broker. No visit. The room and the people are judged only from what is on the screen.

A person who was chosen, and later turns out to be difficult, is not a failed roomeo. An assigned person is.

**Not this product.** Not a compatibility score. Not "shut every PG." Not one contract that bundles everyone's rent. Not a rule that the group must be three. Three was an example of more than one. Not a portal where the rest of the deal happens on WhatsApp.

## 4. Who it is for

**Seeker.** One adult. A student new to the city, a first job, or someone leaving a PG, all fit. They may come from any city. The bed is in Bengaluru. They will stay at least a month. They start alone and choose others, or they arrive with friends who are each that same kind of person.

**Owner.** Empty beds in Bengaluru. Three shapes, same close: lives in the house, owns it but lives elsewhere, or runs a small PG they already operate. If they live elsewhere, they still tap yes themselves. Only the owner publishes. Only the owner says yes.

**One account, two hats.** A person may list a bed and also join someone else's group. They cannot join the group on their own bed.

**Manager.** May draft a listing. Cannot publish. Cannot say yes. Cannot touch money.

**Out.** Couples. Families. Anyone who wants the whole flat alone. Stays under a month. Brokers. A manager acting as the owner. A chain, even if it promises to follow the rules. A second city.

**Bed rule.** One paying person per bed. Friends do not share a room. A couple is out because a couple is a household.

## 5. Where it sits

Not a broker, and not a PG brand. A bed in Bengaluru, a group that chose itself, each share paid here, ₹499 once.

If someone says it is NoBroker for flatmates, the answer is: the group is chosen here, the owner says yes once, and the money does not leave for a broker. The fee and the missing visit are part of that. Neither one is the whole difference.

Do not file it next to a co-living brand, a site for taking a whole flat, or a short stay. From SpareRoom, take only the subject: rooms and the people. Not the busy search, not the filters, not the map, not the viewing.

## 6. First version

**Must.**

- A Bengaluru bed, room or small PG, one paying person, rent visible.
- A profile before anyone can be chosen: name, how long they will stay, that they can pay, a photo, and a short note in their own words.
- A group that chooses its own members. No score. No visit.
- A live message thread inside the product, before the yes.
- An identity check on the owner and on each person in the group, before the yes.
- The owner says yes or no once.
- Each person pays their own bed, inside roomeo.

**The group is finished when it says so,** even if a bed is empty. An empty bed is not a failed close, and it is not billed.

**Waits.** Reviews. A written contract. Another city. A store app. A compatibility score. Booking a visit. A manager saying yes. A deposit. A refund. A staff role.

## 7. The path

**Seeker.** Account, then the profile. They find a Bengaluru bed, or friends bring them in. The people already in the group accept or refuse. They message inside roomeo. They mark the group finished. Each person is checked. The owner says yes or no once. If yes, each person pays for their own bed.

**Owner.** Account, then the identity check, before the bed can be seen. They publish the bed. A manager may have drafted it. They see a finished group and say yes or no once.

**Owner says no.** Nobody pays. The group stays together and can take itself to another bed or another owner. They are not checked again just to change beds.

**One person fails the check, or does not pay.** The others still pay. That seat opens at once. There is no timer. The group chooses the next person. That person is checked. The owner says yes only for them. Money already paid by the others is not returned.

**The next month.** Each person pays their own rent again, on the day they first paid. That date does not move because someone else joined later. The seat is theirs only while that share is paid. Miss it, and the seat opens under the same rule. Someone already in and paying does not need a new yes.

**A new flat, or a new person on a bed,** is a new stay. The fee applies again. The person who stayed is not charged a second fee.

## 8. Money

There is no group payment. Each person pays only for their own bed.

A flat with beds at ₹12,000, ₹10,000, and ₹10,000. A group of two leaves the third empty. The owner says yes. The first pays ₹12,000. The second pays ₹10,000. The empty bed is not billed.

**roomeo's fee.** ₹499 once from the seeker, on top of the rent. ₹499 once from the owner, taken out of their side. Per person, per stay. Not once for the whole flat. Not every month.

On a ₹12,000 bed, month one: the seeker pays ₹12,499, the owner receives ₹11,501. Month two, same person, same flat: the seeker pays ₹12,000, the owner receives ₹12,000. A broker's month on that bed is ₹12,000. ₹499 once still beats it. Two people means the owner pays ₹499 twice, which is still far under one month.

"Free" later months means no second fee. The rent still moves. "One agreement" means that person's paid stay, not a written contract.

No staff. No refund. If someone leaves after paying, the ₹499 stays. A failed check cannot be waived.

Rent moves by UPI or card, through a payments partner. roomeo does not build a bank.

## 9. Identity

A machine reads a photo of an ID. It keeps pass or fail, and deletes the photo and the number. The owner sees the result, not the ID. The full ID number is not stored.

A fail stays a fail. One automatic second try is allowed. Still no human. A second fail stays a fail. A false fail never reaches the money, because the check is before the yes. A false pass can pay, and there is no refund.

The owner is checked before their bed can be seen. Each seeker is checked before the owner says yes to that group.

## 10. How it feels

The first line is sharp: ₹499, not a broker's month. After that, the screen is plain. Rent, names, and the next step. Almost no other slogan.

Modern and young. Fun leads, in pink, purple, and blue. The bright color is the main color, not a small dot. Exact shades are not chosen. Trust shows up as clear numbers and an obvious next step, not as a serious skin. It may look as young as a PG brand. It must not become a swipe screen, a score, a filter wall, a map, or a broker's office.

The old warm paper, deep green, and gold are out.

## 11. What it is built on

A phone site. Bengaluru only. Messages feel live: a new one shows without leaving and coming back.

**Out of the build.** A store app. A map. Live location. Cash. A broker. Operating a building or a PG. A chain as supply. Storing an ID number or the ID photo after the check. A second city, including as a way to pad the number. A human who can touch a payment or reverse a failed check.

## 12. Success

**The win.** Fifty Bengaluru beds pay a second month here. No second fee on those payments. A group of one counts. An empty bed does not. A first payment does not. A signup does not. A bed outside Bengaluru does not.

**Before the first rupee, all four.** Ten real Bengaluru conversations, with owners and with seekers. Both sides accept the same rent on a real bed. The payments partner can take UPI or a card. The identity check can really pass or fail. If any one is missing, the rupee is not real.

**Watched every week, and not called success.** Beds published. Seekers who sent a message. Groups that died at the identity check. Groups that died because the owner said no. First payments that never became a second month.

## 13. What kills it, and what reopens it

**Kills the bet.** People still pay a broker a month outside. Owners will not say yes without a visit. Seekers will not photograph an ID. Any one of the three.

**Will not accept.** A rupee before the four gates. A chain listing beds. Anyone but the seeker and the owner touching the money. roomeo operating a building or a PG.

**Reopen the promise if.** Owners demand a visit. Seekers refuse the ID photo. People still pay a broker outside. The screen starts scoring people, or gets used like a dating app.

**If Bengaluru stays thin.** Wait. Do not open another city. A second city is unset.

## 14. Accepted, and not a defect

Owners are assumed to say yes with no visit. Seekers are assumed to photograph an ID. People are assumed to choose from a photo and a note. ₹499 once is assumed to beat going back to a broker. A room photo can lie. A chosen person can turn out to be difficult. None of that, by itself, means roomeo failed.
